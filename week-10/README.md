# Week 10: Field Note

[Link to website](https://erdemeeesgithub.github.io/f26-ima/week-10/index_hw.html)

I made a little horror Ouija board. You type a question, the lights flicker while the spirit is thinking, and then it spells out the answer letter by letter. The answers come from the Advice Slip API, so I don’t know what it’s gonna say until it comes back.

I tested what happens when it breaks by changing the URL to a wrong one. It says “no one answered. the room went cold.” I liked that even the error feels like part of the story.

The advice from the API isn’t scary at all, it’s just random advice. But when it gets spelled out slowly on a Ouija board, it feels creepy. So the way you show the info really changes how it feels.

It was a little confusing, so I used W3Schools and AI to help me understand fetch and async/await.

For the final project, I will continue to work on my portfolio making it more interactable also adding loading screen.
