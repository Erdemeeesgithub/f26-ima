Meet Freddy and his friends!

Freddy Fazbear

Based on the Freddy Fazbear's Pizza theme song, during the day, Freddy loves to have fun and is also shown to be laid-back and easygoing

Bonnie The Bunny

The Freddy Fazbear's Pizza Theme Song reveals Bonnie to have a personality based on a rock musician: prideful.

Chica The Chicken

Based on the Freddy Fazbear's Pizza Theme Song, Chica is a pushy but lovable singing animatronic bot who has love for food, especially pizza.

Foxy The Pirate Fox

Based on Freddy's description of Foxy in the Theme Song, Freddy Fazbear sings about Foxy rather than Foxy singing himself.
